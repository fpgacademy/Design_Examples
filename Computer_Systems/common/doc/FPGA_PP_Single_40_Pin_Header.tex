\subsubsection{Expansion Parallel Port}

The \systemName~includes a bidirectional parallel port that is connected to the
40-pin {\it \expansionPortA} expansion header on the \DEBoard~board. This parallel port
includes the four 32-bit registers that were described previously for 
Figure~\ref{fig:parallel_port}. The base address of this port is {\sf 0xFF200060}.
Figure \ref{fig:expansion_port} gives a diagram of 
the {\expansionPortA} expansion connector on the \DEBoard~board, and shows how the 
respective bits $D_{31-0}$ of the {\it Data} register at address {\sf 0xFF200060} 
are assigned to the pins on the connector. The figure shows that bit $D_0$ of
the parallel port is assigned to Pin 2 at the top-right corner of the
connector, bit $D_1$ is assigned to Pin 4, $D_2$ to Pin 5, and so on. There are 36 data pins
on {\it \expansionPortA}, and 32 of these are connected to the parallel port.
Pins 12 and 30 on {\it \expansionPortA} are hardwired to ground, Pin 11 to 5V power,
and Pin 29 to 3.3V power.
